Privacy

Version 2026-10-04

No account needed. Every feature — spinning for a dish, growing crops, cooking, check-ins — works without signing in. Your progress then lives only in your browser.

Check-in photos never leave your device. Photos are compressed and stripped of location data (EXIF/GPS) on your device, then kept in your browser.

An account needs only an email — no password, no name, no phone number.

2. Consent and legal basis

When you create an account, you tick a box agreeing that we store your email and progress for syncing (required for this feature). Receiving offers is a separate choice and is off by default. We record when you agreed and which version of this policy you agreed to. Under Vietnam's Decree 13/2023/NĐ-CP on personal data protection, you can withdraw your consent at any time by turning off offers or deleting your account.

3. Links to other services

The Google Maps, GrabFood, ShopeeFood and beFood buttons only open that service's search page with the dish name. We do not send your email or progress to these services; once they open, your data is handled under their own policies.

4. Your rights

  • View and download: Profile → "Download a copy" (data on this device) and "Download my server data" (account data).
  • Change your email preferences: Profile → Account → "Get offers by email".
  • Delete: Profile → "Delete account" permanently deletes your email, progress, garden, friends list and sessions from the server. Seeds or thanks you already sent to friends stay theirs, no longer linked to you (shown as “a former friend”). So that an account cannot be deleted and re-created to collect invite rewards again, we keep a keyed hash of the email (it cannot be turned back into the email) together with the garden that invited it. "Reset progress" deletes the data and photos on this device. Clearing this site's data in your browser also deletes the copy on your device.

5. How long we keep data, and how we protect it

Account data is kept until you delete your account. Sign-in codes expire after 10 minutes; sign-in sessions expire after 180 days. Sign-in codes are stored only as keyed hashes, links and sessions as SHA-256 hashes; connections use HTTPS. To keep the game fair, the server checks every farm save and records the one-time rewards already received and the harvests and cooking it verified; these records are deleted with the account. The server also keeps a log of in-game activity (each XP, xu, seed or ingredient gained or spent, with its time) and of refused saves, with a keyed hash of the IP address, for 90 days and only to detect cheating; the log is in your data download (without the IP hashes) and is deleted when you delete your account.

6. Contact

You can view, download or delete your personal data at any time under Profile → Account: “Download my server data” gives you a copy of everything we store, and “Delete account” permanently removes the account and all its server data; unticking the newsletter box withdraws consent to emails. We will update this page when the way we handle data changes, and note the version date.
